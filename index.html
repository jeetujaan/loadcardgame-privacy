Load Card Game – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Load Card Game does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your real name, email address, phone number or location. This policy explains what the app does handle, which services it uses, and what choices you have.

In this policy, "we" and "the app" mean Load Card Game, published by [Developer / Company Name]. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We collect only what the game needs to run. You can play against bots without any of this leaving your device.

  • Player name and avatar. The nickname and avatar you choose. In Play Hotspot and Play Online they are shown to the other players in your room.
  • Anonymous ID. For Play Online, Firebase Authentication signs you in anonymously and gives your device a random ID. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Game and room data. In Play Online we store the room code, the list of players in the room, ready status, and the moves and cards of the current match, so everyone sees the same game.
  • Device and advertising data. Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and app usage to show and measure ads.
  • Settings saved on your device. Your selected table and card designs, unlocked items, avatar, name and sound setting are stored locally on your phone and are not sent to us.

How we use data, and keeping or deleting it

We use the information above only to run the game: to match and connect players, show names and avatars, sync matches, carry voice chat, send reminders and show ads. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for profiling.

Room data is temporary. It is removed when a room is closed or empties out. The anonymous ID can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data kept on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app.

To ask us to delete anything we hold about you, email us at the address under Contact and include your anonymous ID if you know it.

Children, security, changes and contact

Children. The app is a general-audience card game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security. Online data travels over encrypted connections and is handled by Firebase and Agora. No method of transmission is fully secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes. We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and continued use of the app means you accept the update.
